Referral coordination evidence desk

Prepare complete referral packets and scheduling options while clinicians retain clinical decisions and care priorities.

Explore in the architecture studio

The business situation

Illustrative reference architecture. Validate requirements for your organization.

Illustrative architecture: A hospital network receives specialty referrals with scanned attachments, incomplete records, and differing scheduling requirements. Coordinators need to locate missing administrative evidence without exposing unrelated patient records. This illustrative architecture supports referral operations; it does not diagnose, triage emergencies, or recommend treatment.

Problem

Patient identity mismatches and incomplete referral documentation create repeated handoffs. A model may incorrectly interpret missing information as a negative finding or mistake a scheduling preference for a clinical instruction. Administrative automation must preserve these distinctions.

Why a multi-agent system?

Specialists separately verify record identity, retrieve permitted documents, check locally defined packet requirements, and prepare scheduling choices. Clinical urgency and suitability remain explicit inputs from authorized clinicians, never inferred scheduling shortcuts.

Intended outcome

The intended result is a reviewable operational decision with source evidence and explicit ownership. Measure coordinator effort and documentation completeness alongside privacy and wrong-patient incidents. Scheduling speed alone is insufficient: the workflow must preserve clinician ownership and keep every unresolved item visible until a responsible person closes it.

Architecture diagrams

Runtime flow

  1. 1

    Register referral

    Validate referring organization, patient identifiers, requested specialty, and assigned coordinator before creating a durable referral case.

  2. 2

    Resolve identity

    Check the patient index deterministically; route ambiguous matches to trained staff without gathering candidate charts into a model prompt.

  3. 3

    Retrieve permitted evidence

    Fetch only documents relevant to the referral purpose and capture record versions, provenance, and access decisions.

  4. 4

    Check packet requirements

    Apply the approved administrative checklist; distinguish missing, unavailable, and explicitly documented information in the draft.

  5. 5

    Prepare coordinator choices

    Present outstanding requests and appointment options while displaying clinician-supplied urgency and any unresolved identity issue.

  6. 6

    Commit approved scheduling

    Recheck consent and slot availability, execute an approved booking once, then record the receipt and pending follow-up responsibilities.

Failure recovery

Two patients share similar demographics.

Detect: Identifier checks fail to establish a unique permitted chart match.

Recover: Suspend retrieval and route identity resolution to the hospital's existing registration process.

An appointment slot disappears during review.

Detect: Booking adapter rejects the previously displayed availability version.

Recover: Refresh options and return to the coordinator without silently substituting a different appointment.

Security & decision boundaries

  • Apply patient, organization, and purpose-of-use authorization before every document retrieval.
  • Keep clinical urgency and treatment decisions under qualified clinician control.
  • Show missing data as missing; never generate normal findings from absence.
  • Exclude clinical document text from general application logs and tracing attributes.
  • Bind scheduling approval to patient, specialty, slot, and current permission context.

Operational metrics

Measurement definitions, not promised improvements.

Packet completeness

Referrals meeting the approved administrative checklist divided by referrals submitted for coordinator review.

Identity escalation rate

Referrals requiring manual identity resolution divided by received referrals, monitored by source organization.

Coordination lead time

Elapsed time from referral receipt to a coordinator-approved next step, with patient waiting time reported separately.

Booking reconciliation rate

Booking attempts with uncertain or conflicting receipts divided by approved booking attempts.

Rollout plan

Synthetic referral evaluation

Staff verify identity ambiguity, missing-document labeling, and forbidden clinical advice cases without patient data.

Read-only coordinator pilot

Approved users inspect live packets after access, provenance, and audit checks pass.

Bounded booking assistance

One specialty enables coordinator-approved booking after consent changes and slot-race recovery are demonstrated.
