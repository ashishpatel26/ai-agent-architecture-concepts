Mine maintenance and shift evidence desk

Combine equipment condition, inspection records, and shift constraints into supervisor-reviewed maintenance coordination.

Explore in the architecture studio

The business situation

Illustrative reference architecture. Validate requirements for your organization.

Illustrative architecture: A mine operations team sees recurring haul-truck faults while production plans and workshop capacity change across shifts. Supervisors need to understand equipment evidence, open inspections, parts, and crew qualifications before assigning work. Agents prepare coordination packets without controlling vehicles, ventilation, blasting, or safety systems.

Problem

Fault codes can repeat after an unresolved repair, telemetry links may be intermittent, and shift handoffs lose context. Optimizing maintenance solely around production targets can obscure inspection requirements or assume equipment is available when it remains restricted.

Why a multi-agent system?

Condition, inspection, resource, and handoff specialists reconcile distinct operational records. Deterministic scheduling respects supervisor-approved constraints, and every proposal carries the equipment's authoritative restriction state.

Intended outcome

The intended result is a reviewable operational decision with source evidence and explicit ownership. Measure evidence readiness and task ownership before attributing changes in equipment availability. Physical maintenance and safety decisions remain with qualified operational teams, whose actions should be distinguishable from agent preparation in the record.

Runtime flow

  1. 1

    Register recurring fault

    Deduplicate repeated messages while preserving count, asset identity, operating interval, and current restriction status.

  2. 2

    Check evidence quality

    Identify stale telemetry and distinguish repeated historical codes from newly observed faults.

  3. 3

    Review open obligations

    Retrieve unresolved inspections and previous repair evidence before proposing another maintenance action.

  4. 4

    Evaluate workshop options

    Apply bay, parts, crew, and approved access constraints; retain restriction state as a hard planning condition.

  5. 5

    Obtain supervisor review

    Present candidate work packages, missing evidence, and expected handoff ownership to the accountable supervisor.

  6. 6

    Track approved execution

    Create the authorized work order and shift handoff, then await qualified completion evidence before changing operational availability.

Failure recovery

An offline truck sends old fault events after reconnecting.

Detect: Event timestamps and sequence numbers place the messages before the latest verified repair.

Recover: Retain them as historical evidence and avoid reopening work solely from receipt order.

A shift handoff has no accepting supervisor.

Detect: The handoff remains unacknowledged beyond the configured shift transition window.

Recover: Escalate to the duty manager and keep the previous owner accountable until acceptance is recorded.

Security & decision boundaries

  • Keep vehicle, blasting, ventilation, and safety-control interfaces outside agent authority.
  • Treat equipment restrictions and outstanding inspections as authoritative planning constraints.
  • Require qualified signoff before maintenance completion changes availability status.
  • Preserve event time and device sequence when telemetry reconnects after an outage.
  • Bind work assignments to current crew qualifications and approved procedures.

Operational metrics

Measurement definitions, not promised improvements.

Handoff completeness

Open work items with evidence, owner, and acknowledged next shift divided by handed-off items.

Fault packet latency

Time from accepted new fault to supervisor-reviewable condition and maintenance context.

Stale event rejection

Historical reconnect events correctly excluded from new-fault triggers divided by audited stale events.

Work readiness

Approved packages with required parts, qualified crew, and current procedures divided by approved packages.
