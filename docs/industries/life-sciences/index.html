Clinical study deviation evidence assembly

Assemble protocol deviation evidence for study teams while preserving source records and qualified reviewer decisions.

Explore in the architecture studio

The business situation

Illustrative reference architecture. Validate requirements for your organization.

Illustrative architecture: A clinical study operations team receives visit exceptions across sites, laboratory feeds, and electronic data capture. Reviewers need to distinguish missing data from a potential protocol deviation and document their assessment. The agents assist records management and operational review, not participant care or trial eligibility decisions.

Problem

Protocol amendments differ by site activation date, and late lab results can change the evidence available at review. Flattening these timelines into a summary risks applying the wrong requirement or implying a clinical conclusion unsupported by source data.

Why a multi-agent system?

Specialists independently align protocol versions, reconcile visit events, gather approved source references, and assemble the review packet. Their separation supports explicit evidence ownership and prevents a drafting agent from amending captured clinical observations.

Intended outcome

The intended result is a reviewable operational decision with source evidence and explicit ownership. Evaluate preparation effort together with source traceability and reviewer correction frequency. The proposed architecture does not establish regulatory compliance; study owners must map their actual procedures, systems, and oversight requirements before deployment.

Architecture diagrams

Runtime flow

  1. 1

    Register exception

    Create a study-scoped event using subject pseudonym, site, visit, and source revision; preserve the original signal.

  2. 2

    Resolve protocol interval

    Match the site's activation history to the event date and expose unresolved amendment applicability for reviewer attention.

  3. 3

    Reconcile chronology

    Compare scheduled visits, collection timestamps, receipt timestamps, and late corrections without conflating their meanings.

  4. 4

    Collect evidence

    Retrieve the minimum permitted study records and build a provenance manifest that points back to controlled originals.

  5. 5

    Draft assessment packet

    Describe the possible deviation, missing evidence, and contradictory facts; reserve clinical significance and final classification for qualified reviewers.

  6. 6

    Record disposition

    Save the approved assessment with reviewer identity and source versions; subsequent source corrections reopen dependent review sections.

Failure recovery

A site activation date is missing for an amendment.

Detect: Protocol interval resolver cannot select one valid version.

Recover: Block protocol comparison and assign the unresolved date to study operations before drafting conclusions.

A laboratory result arrives after the packet was signed.

Detect: Source event version advances for an evidence item linked to the assessment.

Recover: Notify the responsible reviewer, preserve the signed record, and open a linked amendment assessment.

Security & decision boundaries

  • Use study pseudonyms in prompts and isolate reidentification mappings from agent access.
  • Enforce site and study entitlements on all source queries and exports.
  • Preserve source records and corrections; agent drafts never overwrite captured observations.
  • Require qualified review of deviation classification and any participant-impacting decision.
  • Record amendment version, site activation interval, and evidence cutoff in every packet.

Operational metrics

Measurement definitions, not promised improvements.

Evidence traceability

Reviewed packet assertions with resolvable controlled source references divided by audited assertions.

Version resolution rate

Exceptions with unambiguous applicable protocol versions divided by all registered exceptions.

Review preparation time

Elapsed time from exception registration to reviewer-ready chronology, segmented by site and event class.

Reopened assessment rate

Assessments reopened for late or corrected evidence divided by completed assessments.

Rollout plan

Archived study exercise

Reviewers validate amendment selection and chronology on representative exceptions with known dispositions.

Read-only site pilot

One study permits draft packets after pseudonymization, source access, and correction-history tests pass.

Controlled quality integration

Approved dispositions enter the quality system only after late-evidence reopening and audit reconstruction are demonstrated.
