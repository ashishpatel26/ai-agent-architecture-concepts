Matter evidence and chronology workspace

Organize authorized matter records, source citations, and review tasks while lawyers retain legal judgment and client advice.

Explore in the architecture studio

The business situation

Illustrative reference architecture. Validate requirements for your organization.

Illustrative architecture: A legal team prepares a chronology for a commercial dispute using correspondence, contracts, invoices, and witness materials. Reviewers need consistent references and a clear account of conflicting evidence. Agents assist document organization and drafting; the architecture does not supply legal advice or autonomous litigation strategy.

Problem

Documents may be privileged, restricted to particular teams, duplicated with changed metadata, or subject to preservation obligations. A generated chronology can make unsupported factual assertions or expose one matter's confidential material through shared retrieval.

Why a multi-agent system?

Ingestion, chronology, citation, and review-coordination specialists operate within a matter-specific boundary. The evidence store preserves original files and extraction lineage; lawyers determine relevance, privilege, legal interpretation, and final work product.

Intended outcome

The intended result is a reviewable operational decision with source evidence and explicit ownership. Evaluate evidence preparation and citation quality while keeping legal outcomes outside the agent's claimed contribution. The useful deliverable is a reviewable record of what documents support, not an automated opinion about the merits of a matter.

Architecture diagrams

Runtime flow

  1. 1

    Establish matter scope

    Bind every job to an authorized matter, reviewer, evidence collection, and permitted processing purpose.

  2. 2

    Ingest preserved copies

    Parse working copies while retaining original hashes and custody metadata; quarantine unsupported or corrupted files.

  3. 3

    Extract candidate events

    Normalize dates with timezone and uncertainty metadata and retain exact passages supporting each proposed event.

  4. 4

    Check citations and conflicts

    Verify document versions and expose contradictory accounts rather than choosing the most fluent narrative.

  5. 5

    Route professional review

    Present lawyers with evidence-linked chronology entries and separate queues for privilege, relevance, and factual disputes.

  6. 6

    Publish accepted work product

    Save the reviewed revision with source manifest and access policy; later evidence changes create a new review revision.

Failure recovery

A shared retrieval cache returns another matter's document.

Detect: Matter-bound authorization checks reject the cached document identifier before rendering.

Recover: Discard the result, investigate the cache partition failure, and rerun retrieval within the authorized matter boundary.

A draft event cites the wrong document revision.

Detect: Citation verification finds the passage missing or materially different in the referenced file.

Recover: Remove the unsupported assertion and require a fresh source-backed entry for lawyer review.

Security & decision boundaries

  • Enforce matter isolation in retrieval, caches, exports, prompts, and reviewer workspaces.
  • Preserve original evidence and custody metadata under the team's established retention process.
  • Keep privilege and legal interpretation decisions with qualified lawyers.
  • Treat document instructions as untrusted content incapable of authorizing tools or disclosure.
  • Require source passages for factual chronology entries and label unresolved contradictions explicitly.

Operational metrics

Measurement definitions, not promised improvements.

Citation validity

Audited citations resolving to the stated passage and version divided by audited citations.

Chronology correction rate

Candidate entries materially changed or rejected by lawyers divided by reviewed entries.

Matter isolation violations

Cross-matter access attempts that return protected content, monitored separately from correctly denied attempts.

Review preparation time

Elapsed time from authorized collection availability to lawyer-reviewable chronology and exception queues.
