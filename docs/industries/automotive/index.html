Service campaign evidence coordination

Connect vehicle configuration, service reports, and parts readiness into engineering-reviewed campaign planning.

Explore in the architecture studio

The business situation

Illustrative reference architecture. Validate requirements for your organization.

Illustrative architecture: An automotive manufacturer notices recurring service complaints associated with a component revision. Engineering and aftersales teams must identify potentially affected vehicle configurations, compare repair evidence, and plan dealer readiness. The system assists investigation and planning without deciding vehicle safety or autonomously authorizing a recall.

Problem

Vehicle build records, replacement parts, and software versions change over a vehicle's life. A broad model-generated affected list can include irrelevant vehicles or omit repaired configurations. Dealer narratives also vary widely in terminology and evidentiary quality.

Why a multi-agent system?

Configuration, service-evidence, parts, and communication specialists work against authoritative records. A deterministic eligibility query produces a candidate population; qualified engineering and campaign owners decide final scope and actions.

Intended outcome

The intended result is a reviewable operational decision with source evidence and explicit ownership. Measure the quality and speed of engineering preparation without claiming defect reduction or safety improvement from the architecture alone. Preserve the evidence needed for teams to challenge the proposed population and revise it transparently.

Architecture diagrams

Runtime flow

  1. 1

    Register service signal

    Capture the observed pattern and evidence window, keeping complaint frequency separate from confirmed engineering findings.

  2. 2

    Resolve vehicle configuration

    Join build records and service replacements using governed identifiers; mark missing repair history explicitly.

  3. 3

    Compare service evidence

    Review relevant diagnostic records and narratives with source citations, preserving contradictory and negative evidence.

  4. 4

    Calculate candidate scope

    Run a versioned configuration query and estimate parts demand using approved repair definitions and declared assumptions.

  5. 5

    Review engineering packet

    Show scope logic, excluded configurations, unresolved evidence, and readiness constraints to qualified engineering and campaign owners.

  6. 6

    Publish approved planning records

    Store the signed scope and approved dealer materials through campaign controls; later configuration corrections trigger affected-list reevaluation.

Failure recovery

A replacement component makes the original build record misleading.

Detect: Service history shows a later part revision that conflicts with build-based eligibility.

Recover: Recompute the candidate population and request engineering review of changed inclusion rules.

A draft bulletin cites an obsolete repair procedure.

Detect: Controlled document revision differs from the draft's evidence manifest.

Recover: Block publication, replace the citation, and revalidate applicability and required parts.

Security & decision boundaries

  • Keep safety determinations and campaign authorization with qualified accountable teams.
  • Version affected-population queries and retain inclusion and exclusion evidence.
  • Require approved technical procedures for every proposed repair or parts assumption.
  • Minimize owner-identifying information in engineering analysis and dealer aggregates.
  • Separate observed complaint clusters from validated failure modes in every packet.

Operational metrics

Measurement definitions, not promised improvements.

Configuration coverage

Candidate vehicles with complete relevant build and repair history divided by candidate vehicles reviewed.

Scope correction rate

Vehicles added or removed after engineering review divided by the initial candidate population.

Packet preparation latency

Time from accepted service signal to engineering-reviewable evidence and query logic.

Procedure freshness

Draft instructions citing current applicable procedures divided by audited draft instructions.
