Travel and Hospitality · INDUSTRY BLUEPRINT

Disruption-aware guest journey recovery

Coordinate booking, inventory, accessibility requirements, and supplier policies before confirming a traveler-approved change.

Explore in the architecture studio

The business situation

Illustrative reference architecture. Validate requirements for your organization.

Illustrative architecture: A travel operator manages guests whose delayed arrival affects hotel check-in, airport transfers, and a prepaid activity. Service staff need compatible alternatives across suppliers. Agents prepare options with clear terms; travelers and authorized staff approve changes and financial commitments.

Problem

Availability expires quickly, supplier confirmation can lag, and one successful cancellation may leave another service unchanged. An agent that says the journey is fixed after a single receipt can leave the guest stranded or double booked.

Why a multi-agent system?

Booking, inventory, policy, and itinerary specialists work on separate supplier interfaces. A durable coordinator owns reservation expiry, customer consent, and partial-success recovery across the journey.

Intended outcome

The intended result is a reviewable operational decision with source evidence and explicit ownership. Evaluate confirmed recovery quality and repeat contacts alongside preparation time. Guest satisfaction depends on supplier execution and individual needs, so retain requirement-level evidence and unresolved obligations throughout the case.

Architecture diagrams

Runtime flow

  1. 1

    Register disruption

    Verify traveler identity and link affected itinerary components with current confirmations and remaining service deadlines.

  2. 2

    Read constraints

    Retrieve explicit accessibility, timing, occupancy, and change-policy requirements without inferring sensitive preferences.

  3. 3

    Find compatible options

    Query suppliers in parallel and label unheld quotes, held inventory, and confirmed bookings distinctly.

  4. 4

    Price complete change

    Calculate total fees and refund timing across components, including the consequences if only part of the plan succeeds.

  5. 5

    Confirm traveler choice

    Present exact services, times, accessibility confirmations, amounts, and expiry before recording consent.

  6. 6

    Execute coordinated recovery

    Commit held components in the approved order, reconcile acknowledgements, and escalate or compensate partial changes under the agreed policy.

Failure recovery

A hotel hold expires while the transfer change succeeds.

Detect: Supplier receipts show a confirmed transfer but rejected accommodation commitment.

Recover: Keep the case open, present remaining options, and apply only the compensation actions the traveler approved.

An accessibility attribute is unverified.

Detect: Supplier data lacks the required confirmed attribute or conflicts with the guest's requirement.

Recover: Request supplier confirmation and exclude the option from confirmed-compatible choices until resolved.

Security & decision boundaries

  • Require traveler consent bound to exact itinerary components, prices, and change terms.
  • Keep accessibility requirements explicit and verify supplier evidence before making commitments.
  • Protect passport and identity data from unnecessary specialist prompts and logs.
  • Track each supplier operation with its own idempotency key and confirmation state.
  • Never describe a partially completed journey change as fully confirmed.

Operational metrics

Measurement definitions, not promised improvements.

Compatible option latency

Time from verified disruption to a fully priced option satisfying recorded requirements.

Confirmation completeness

Approved changes with all required supplier receipts divided by approved changes.

Partial recovery duration

Time from first partial failure to traveler-accepted resolution or accountable escalation.

Requirement fidelity

Audited booked options meeting all explicitly recorded requirements divided by audited booked options.
