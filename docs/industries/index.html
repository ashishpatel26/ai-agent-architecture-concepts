Industry architecture blueprints

Aerospace: Aircraft maintenance records readiness

Assemble configuration-sensitive maintenance evidence for licensed personnel without granting release-to-service authority.

Agriculture: Field operations planning assistant

Combine field observations, weather, equipment, and agronomist-approved practices into reviewable farm work plans.

Automotive: Service campaign evidence coordination

Connect vehicle configuration, service reports, and parts readiness into engineering-reviewed campaign planning.

Banking: Payment exception investigation desk

Reconcile payment evidence across ledgers, screening queues, and customer records while keeping release authority with bank operations.

Capital Markets: Trade settlement break resolution

Investigate unmatched allocations and settlement instructions without granting agents trading or settlement authority.

Construction: Design change and site impact coordination

Trace a design revision through quantities, procurement, and site sequencing before project teams approve a change.

E-commerce: Order recovery and returns coordination

Coordinate order status, shipment evidence, and return eligibility before committing a customer-approved resolution.

Education: Student support case coordination

Coordinate academic records, support resources, and appointment options while staff retain consequential student decisions.

Energy: Renewable asset maintenance planning

Combine turbine condition evidence, weather windows, and spare-part availability into engineer-approved maintenance plans.

Enterprise Software: Tenant-scoped incident and change assistant

Combine tenant symptoms, release history, and bounded diagnostics into engineer-approved incident recovery.

Government: Public service application completeness desk

Assemble application evidence and service-rule citations for accountable caseworker review with accessible applicant communication.

Healthcare: Referral coordination evidence desk

Prepare complete referral packets and scheduling options while clinicians retain clinical decisions and care priorities.

Insurance: Property claim evidence workbench

Coordinate damage evidence, coverage documents, and repair estimates into an adjuster-owned claim review.

Legal Services: Matter evidence and chronology workspace

Organize authorized matter records, source citations, and review tasks while lawyers retain legal judgment and client advice.

Life Sciences: Clinical study deviation evidence assembly

Assemble protocol deviation evidence for study teams while preserving source records and qualified reviewer decisions.

Logistics: Shipment exception recovery desk

Coordinate carrier evidence, dock capacity, and customer commitments for dispatcher-approved shipment recovery.

Manufacturing: Production quality containment assistant

Connect quality signals, lot genealogy, and maintenance evidence before a supervisor approves containment work.

Maritime: Port call readiness and exception coordination

Reconcile vessel arrival estimates, berth constraints, cargo readiness, and agency documents for port-operator decisions.

Media and Publishing: Editorial evidence and rights coordination

Assemble source verification, asset rights, and publication checks for editor-owned release decisions.

Mining: Mine maintenance and shift evidence desk

Combine equipment condition, inspection records, and shift constraints into supervisor-reviewed maintenance coordination.

Nonprofits: Grant delivery and beneficiary evidence desk

Coordinate restricted funding, program evidence, and reporting obligations while staff retain funding and beneficiary decisions.

Professional Services: Engagement delivery evidence coordinator

Connect client requirements, delivery evidence, staffing constraints, and commercial scope before engagement leaders commit changes.

Real Estate: Commercial lease operations workbench

Reconcile lease obligations, maintenance requests, and property records for manager-reviewed operational action.

Retail: Store replenishment exception desk

Reconcile shelf observations, inventory records, and delivery plans into manager-approved replenishment actions.

Supply Chain: Supplier disruption response planning

Connect supplier incidents to material demand, qualified alternatives, and planner-approved recovery scenarios.

Telecommunications: Network incident evidence coordination

Correlate alarms, service dependencies, and change history before an operator approves network remediation.

Travel and Hospitality: Disruption-aware guest journey recovery

Coordinate booking, inventory, accessibility requirements, and supplier policies before confirming a traveler-approved change.

Utilities: Water network incident coordination

Assemble pressure, asset, and customer-impact evidence for operator-owned water-network response.
