Supplier disruption response planning

Connect supplier incidents to material demand, qualified alternatives, and planner-approved recovery scenarios.

Explore in the architecture studio

The business situation

Illustrative reference architecture. Validate requirements for your organization.

Illustrative architecture: A machinery manufacturer receives notice that a supplier cannot deliver a critical bearing on time. Procurement, production planning, and engineering need a shared view of affected assemblies and feasible responses. Agents prepare scenarios; planners and engineering owners authorize sourcing and schedule changes.

Problem

Supplier messages use inconsistent part descriptions and uncertain dates. A superficially cheaper substitute may lack engineering approval, and a rescheduled purchase order may disrupt another plant. Local optimization can therefore hide enterprise-wide constraints.

Why a multi-agent system?

Specialists map supply exposure, inspect approved alternatives, simulate planning scenarios, and prepare supplier questions. Structured optimization and material requirements calculations remain deterministic, with agents interpreting exceptions and explaining scenario assumptions.

Intended outcome

The intended result is a reviewable operational decision with source evidence and explicit ownership. Evaluate the completeness and usability of recovery options before attributing service-level improvements. Record where human negotiation, engineering qualification, or physical capacity constrained the result so agent quality is measured separately.

Architecture diagrams

Runtime flow

  1. 1

    Register disruption

    Authenticate the supplier event, map supplier part numbers, and record uncertain delivery windows without choosing a convenient date.

  2. 2

    Calculate exposure

    Read a consistent planning snapshot and identify assemblies, plants, and customer commitments that depend on the material.

  3. 3

    Check alternatives

    Filter candidate substitutes by engineering approval, effective revision, qualification, and available capacity.

  4. 4

    Simulate responses

    Evaluate approved alternatives, transfers, and schedule changes with deterministic constraints and clearly labeled scenario assumptions.

  5. 5

    Review cross-plant effects

    Show planners which shortages move elsewhere and which assumptions remain unconfirmed before a buyer authorizes action.

  6. 6

    Commit selected plan

    Revalidate inventory and order versions, submit approved changes through ERP adapters, and monitor supplier acknowledgement separately from execution.

Failure recovery

A substitute is approved for an older assembly revision only.

Detect: Qualification lookup conflicts with the current bill-of-materials revision.

Recover: Remove that scenario and request engineering assessment without inventing equivalence.

Two plants reserve the same emergency inventory.

Detect: Reservation conflict or changed available-to-promise quantity rejects the second plan.

Recover: Recalculate both affected scenarios using the current allocation and return ownership to planners.

Security & decision boundaries

  • Use governed part identifiers and engineering revision checks before scenario generation.
  • Keep optimization assumptions and supplier-confirmed commitments visibly separate.
  • Require buyer approval for commercial commitments and engineering approval for substitutions.
  • Reserve scarce inventory atomically before publishing a committed recovery plan.
  • Restrict supplier pricing and contracts to authorized procurement teams.

Operational metrics

Measurement definitions, not promised improvements.

Exposure completeness

Known affected demand lines captured in the disruption graph divided by audited affected demand lines.

Scenario preparation latency

Time from authenticated notice to planner-reviewable options with current constraints.

Assumption closure

Unconfirmed scenario assumptions resolved before commitment divided by assumptions requiring confirmation.

Plan invalidation rate

Approved scenarios requiring revision because source constraints changed divided by approved scenarios.
