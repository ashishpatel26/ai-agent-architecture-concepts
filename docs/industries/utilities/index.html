Water network incident coordination

Assemble pressure, asset, and customer-impact evidence for operator-owned water-network response.

Explore in the architecture studio

The business situation

Illustrative reference architecture. Validate requirements for your organization.

Illustrative architecture: A water utility receives low-pressure alarms and customer reports across several neighborhoods. Operators need to reconcile telemetry, planned works, pipe topology, and field observations before dispatching crews or changing network configuration. Agents prepare situational evidence without operating pumps, valves, or public-health decisions.

Problem

Customer reports may be duplicated or geocoded incorrectly, while telemetry can fail during the same incident. Inferring a pipe break from a pressure pattern alone can misdirect crews. Network topology and isolation boundaries require engineering validation.

Why a multi-agent system?

Telemetry, asset-graph, field-work, and customer-impact specialists investigate different evidence channels. The workflow keeps confirmed observations separate from modeled exposure and routes consequential decisions to established utility roles.

Intended outcome

The intended result is a reviewable operational decision with source evidence and explicit ownership. Assess evidence quality and coordination time alongside field response. Service restoration and public-health outcomes require accountable operational decisions; the agent contribution is an inspectable, current picture of what is known and what still needs verification.

Architecture diagrams

Runtime flow

  1. 1

    Correlate incoming reports

    Deduplicate customer cases and alarms by location and time while retaining individual evidence and uncertain addresses.

  2. 2

    Check telemetry reliability

    Compare sensor health, neighboring observations, and recent operational changes before accepting a pressure pattern.

  3. 3

    Map possible exposure

    Trace affected segments using current asset data and label modeled impact separately from confirmed service disruption.

  4. 4

    Gather field context

    Retrieve active works and verified crew observations; surface conflicts between map records and physical findings.

  5. 5

    Prepare operator options

    Show inspection priorities, uncertain isolation assumptions, and a draft customer update for the appropriate operational owner.

  6. 6

    Track authorized response

    Record approved dispatch and communication receipts, then reconcile field findings and telemetry before closing the incident.

Failure recovery

A pressure sensor fails during the incident.

Detect: Sensor diagnostics and neighboring measurements disagree with the isolated reading.

Recover: Exclude the suspect signal from confirmed evidence and request field validation without suppressing the incident.

A field crew discovers a valve absent from the map.

Detect: Verified field report conflicts with the asset revision used in the plan.

Recover: Suspend map-dependent recommendations and route topology correction to engineering before further isolation planning.

Security & decision boundaries

  • Prevent agent access to pump, valve, and supervisory-control write interfaces.
  • Require operational approval for dispatch and separate authorized review for public-health messages.
  • Label confirmed outages, customer reports, and modeled exposure as distinct evidence classes.
  • Protect customer addresses in aggregated incident views and telemetry exports.
  • Attach asset-map revision and field verification status to every network-dependent proposal.

Operational metrics

Measurement definitions, not promised improvements.

Situational packet latency

Time from incident registration to operator-reviewable evidence and proposed next checks.

Location resolution quality

Audited reports assigned to the correct service area divided by audited geocoded reports.

Field contradiction rate

Plans requiring revision after verified field findings divided by plans reviewed in the field.

Communication consistency

Published updates matching approved incident state divided by sampled published updates.
